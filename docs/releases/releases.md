## Latest: v3.1.0 — October 9, 2026

A feature release: your own Claude Code or Codex can now run TI-Toolbox for you, from a new
**Assistant** page or your own terminal, and every plan it proposes waits on **Jobs** for your
approval. Also new: update notifications, carrier-only (ΔF = 0) control simulations, and fsaverage
projections and surface statistics as viewable `.msh` files (re-run older fsaverage projections and
surface analyses), plus Windows path fixes for FreeSurfer, QSIPrep and saved scenes.
