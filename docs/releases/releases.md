## Latest: v3.0.2 — September 28, 2026

A patch release: DTI conductivity tensors placed and oriented correctly (re-run **Extract DTI
tensor** and any `vn`, `dir` or `mc` simulations), QSIRecon no longer needed for the tensor, new
lean reports for simulations, flex-search, ex-search and DTI QC, and charm no longer freezing on
many-core machines.
