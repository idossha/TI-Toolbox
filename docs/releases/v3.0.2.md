# Release v3.0.2

**Release Date**: September 28, 2026

A patch release for everyone on v3.0.x. Install the new desktop app; it downloads the matching
image (`idossha/ti-toolbox:v3.0.2`) on its next start. Update both together: the image carries the
new DTI fit (it adds DIPY) and the server the app talks to. Projects, saved scenes and results from
v3.0.1 open unchanged, but **anisotropic (DTI) simulations need a re-run** (see the first item).

#### Highlights

- **DTI conductivity tensors are placed and oriented correctly.** The tensor TI-Toolbox wrote into
  `m2m_<id>/DTI_coregT1_tensor.nii.gz` was aligned to the head model by a shift only, although
  QSIPrep's space is also rotated (about 21° on the test subject), and the tensors were not rotated
  with it. On the test subject they sat 18 mm off on average, with a 38° median error in fibre
  direction. **Extract DTI tensor** now fits the tensor itself with DIPY from QSIPrep output and maps
  it with QSIPrep's exact transforms, rotating every tensor. A quality gate refuses to write a tensor
  that fails its checks and records the result in `DTI_coregT1_qc.json`.
  - **Who is affected:** simulations using the `vn`, `dir` or `mc` conductivity model, run with
    v2.3.0 through v3.0.1. Isotropic (`scalar`) results are unchanged.
  - **What to do:** run **Pre-processing ▸ Extract DTI tensor** again with **Replace and rerun**,
    then re-run those simulations. Your existing QSIPrep output is reused; QSIPrep does not need to
    run again.

- **QSIRecon is optional.** **Extract DTI tensor** now runs straight after QSIPrep (and charm).
  QSIRecon is only needed for tractography, scalar maps or connectivity. QSIPrep also picks its
  settings from your data: distortion correction is always on (TOPUP with a reverse phase-encoding
  fieldmap, otherwise fieldmap-less SyN), unringing is `rpg` for partial-Fourier data and
  `mrdegibbs` otherwise, and output stays at the DWI's native resolution. Every option remains in
  **Configure QSIPrep**. On Apple Silicon, QSIPrep now refuses to start instead of failing an hour
  in at SynthSeg, and explains how to run it on an x86-64 Linux or Windows machine and bring the
  output back; the DTI step itself runs anywhere.
- **New reports: lean, cited, offline, and in the app's theme.** Every simulation writes a
  **Simulation report** (envelope in grey matter, where its maximum is, the montage on the EEG cap,
  three slices through the hot spot). The **Flex-search report** names the target, says what the
  score means in words ("target mean 1.82× the background mean") and is about 0.3 MB instead of
  7.4 MB. **Ex-search** now writes a report: the winning montage on the cap, every montage in one
  chart, and a sortable top 25. The **DTI QC report** is redesigned around one cited gate and is
  written even when the gate fails. The head-model report is SimNIBS's own **charm report**, copied
  into the subject's reports. All are under **Results ▸ sub-…** and on the job in **Jobs**.

#### Also fixed

- Head-model creation (charm) could freeze indefinitely on machines with many CPU cores (an
  OpenBLAS threading deadlock). It now finishes.

#### Removed (script users)

- The combined preprocessing report (`pre_processing_report_*.html`) and the `report` job kind.
  Reports already on disk stay listed in **Results**.
- The report-building classes in `tit.reporting` (`SimulationReportGenerator`,
  `FlexSearchReportGenerator`, `ReportAssembler`, the reportlets, `PreprocessingReportGenerator`,
  `create_preprocessing_report`), print layouts for reports, and
  `tit.plotting.generate_static_overlay_images`. To rebuild a report from existing outputs, use
  `tit.reporting.generators.<name>` or `simnibs_python -m tit.reporting.generators.<name>`
  (`simulation`, `flex_search`, `ex_search`, `dti_qc`).

The full list is in the [Changelog](https://idossha.github.io/TI-Toolbox/releases/changelog/).
