# Release v3.1.3

**Release Date**: November 3, 2026

A patch release: simulations save less and finish sooner, the envelope maths is exact, and
analyses report how focal the stimulation is as well as the modulation.

**Update:** install the new desktop app; it downloads the matching image
(`idossha/ti-toolbox:v3.1.3`) on its next start. Update both together, since the image carries the
server the app talks to. If you run the image yourself, pull it again:
`docker pull --platform linux/amd64 idossha/ti-toolbox:v3.1.3`.

#### What changed

- **Leaner, faster simulations.** A simulation saves `TI_max`, `hf_peak` and `hf_rms` by default,
  in one field mesh and one cortical surface mesh, and writes NIfTI volumes only when you tick
  **Write NIfTI volumes**. A later step that needs a field or a NIfTI that was not saved computes
  it from the simulation and saves it.
- **`hf_rms` replaces `hf_sar`.** The carrier field is saved as its RMS over time, in V/m.
- **Exact envelope maths.** `mTI_max`, mEx-search scores, 2-channel `TI_avg` and the tangential
  flex-search goal are now computed exactly (see below for what to re-run).
- **Stimulation focality beside modulation focality.** Analyses and Ex/mEx-search results also
  report how focal the carrier field is, and the TI selectivity (above 1, the envelope is more
  focal than its carriers). Existing statistics and rankings are unchanged.
- **Electrodes.** New jobs put electrodes on 1 mm of gel instead of 4 mm, as SimNIBS does for
  flex-search electrodes. You choose the electrode before generating a leadfield, and the
  leadfield records it.
- **Channels and the 3-D view.** Channel k is electrodes Ek+ and Ek- everywhere. Pick a new
  montage's electrodes by clicking them on the head; electrodes stand on the scalp, and
  **Actual size** shows them at their real size on their gel.
- **Your own Simulator defaults** in **Settings ▸ Simulator**, used by every new Simulator job.
- **Multipolar TI** takes at most 12 channels (24 electrodes).
- **Many bugs were squashed**, across group statistics, the analyzer, EEG forward models, the
  optimizer, job scheduling, the desktop app and the scripting API.

#### Do I need to re-run anything?

- **mTI simulations and mEx-searches:** `mTI_max` and mEx scores were slightly low in places (the
  grey-matter mean moves by under 0.1 %). Re-run them if exact element values matter.
- **2-channel `TI_avg`:** element values move by up to 4.6 % (the grey-matter mean by under 0.5 %).
- **Flex-searches with the tangential goal** (`dir_TI_tangential`) optimised the wrong quantity:
  re-run them.
- **Group comparisons and EEG forward models** can give different results: re-run them.
- **Cluster permutations:** a cluster's p-value could come out one permutation count too small;
  re-run one whose p sits just below alpha.
- **Visual Exporter:** re-export `TI_normal.ply`; its arrows are now the simulation's `TI_normal`.
- **Leadfields** generated before this release used SimNIBS's own electrode rather than your
  Simulator's (they show "electrode not recorded"); generate them again to match.

`TI_max`, `TI_normal` and `hf_peak` (up to 8 channels) are unchanged; rescale nothing. A new
simulation that relies on the default gel differs from one run before; set the gel to 4 mm to
reproduce it.

**Scripts:** `tit.calc` is now `envelope`, `envelope_max`, `envelope_mean`, `hf_peak` and
`hf_power` (`get_TI_vectors`, `get_TI_avg`, `get_TI_dir`, `psi` and `tit.fields` are gone); read
`hf_rms` instead of `hf_sar` (`hf_sar` = 2 × `hf_rms`²); and drop `map_to_surf`,
`tissues_in_niftis` and `open_in_gmsh` from `SimulationConfig`.

The full list is in the [Changelog](https://idossha.github.io/TI-Toolbox/releases/changelog/#v313--november-3-2026).
